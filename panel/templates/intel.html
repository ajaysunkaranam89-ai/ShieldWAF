CrowdSec

Use the community blocklist and the decisions of your own CrowdSec server: this panel acts as a bouncer, pulls the ban decisions every 30 seconds and refuses those addresses together with the feeds above. Create the key on the CrowdSec server with cscli bouncers add shieldwaf. The key is stored encrypted and is never shown again.
