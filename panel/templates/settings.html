Settings

Admin panel access

Limit who can even reach the admin panel. Your current address must be on the list (the panel refuses a setting that would lock you out). Recovery if you lose access: kubectl exec -n shieldwaf deploy/shieldwaf -c panel -- python3 /panel/tools.py reset-panel-allow

Upstream restrictions (cloud safety)

Sites may only forward to hosts that end with one of these suffixes, for example svc.cluster.local or your private domain. Empty = any host except loopback / link-local / cloud-metadata addresses (always refused).

Flood defence (layer 7)

If one site receives more requests per second than the threshold, "under attack" mode switches on for that site automatically (everyone must pass the anti-bot challenge, a rate limit applies) and switches off when traffic calms down. This stops application-layer floods. It cannot stop network-level (volumetric) attacks: those must be filtered by your cloud provider or CDN before they reach the cluster (see docs/DDOS.md).

Automatic banning (like fail2ban)

An IP that gets blocked (403) or rate-limited (429) too often is banned automatically on every site.

Real client IP

If A Shield WAF sits behind a cloud load balancer or CDN, list that proxy's CIDRs here so the real client IP is read from X-Forwarded-For. Leave empty when clients connect directly: a spoofed header is then ignored.

Single sign-on (OpenID Connect)

Let people sign in with their company account (Microsoft Entra ID, Google, Okta, Keycloak ...). Register this panel at your identity provider as a web application with the redirect address shown below, then fill in the fields. The password login stays available as the emergency way in. Who may sign in is decided here, not by the provider: nobody is allowed until you list a domain or addresses. Two-factor login is the identity provider's job for these accounts.

Automatic HTTPS certificates (Let's Encrypt)

Sites whose certificate source is Let's Encrypt (automatic) get a free certificate and renewals 30 days before it expires. Needs: a public host name that points to this firewall, and port 80 reachable from the internet (the authority checks http://your-host/.well-known/acme-challenge/...). Wildcard names are not supported. Country rules or IP allow-lists on the site can block the authority's check.

Data retention

Metrics token

Prometheus must send Authorization: Bearer <token> to /metrics. A token exists already; generating a new one shows it once and invalidates the old one.
