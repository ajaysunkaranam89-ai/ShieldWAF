Policy approval

With approval switched on, a change to a site (create, edit, delete) is not applied until another administrator approves it. The author can withdraw it; every step is in the audit trail, and every applied version of a site is kept (Sites, History) so it can be restored.

Settings

Administrators: {{ admins }}. Not covered by approval: maintenance and under-attack switches (emergency tools), users, settings. Config import is switched off while approval is on.
